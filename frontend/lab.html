Simulation Lab

Revenue-Contingent Financing Under Volatile Sales

A model-based and simulation study motivated by Vietnamese e-commerce sellers. The contribution is a deterministic, line-by-line-verifiable comparison of fixed-payment and revenue-based financing — it needs no labels and makes no prediction about who defaults.

Research question. When a seller's revenue falls, how does the burden of a fixed instalment differ from a revenue share — and what does that flexibility cost the financier in recovery speed?

Provider recovery — the other side of the trade

Share of the contractual cap recovered by month 12, 18 and 24. Flexibility for the seller is slower recovery for the financier; both are shown so neither is hidden.

How this is measured

Repayment duration and settlement

The final payment is partial. A revenue-share contract collects a fixed share of revenue until the cap is reached, so the last payment is whatever remains — not a full instalment. Multiplying a periodic payment by the duration therefore overstates the total. The total repaid is the contractual cap, exactly.
